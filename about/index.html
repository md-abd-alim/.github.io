My Journey Into Dental SEO

My interest in SEO comes from understanding how people search for services, compare businesses, and make decisions online.

When patients look for a dentist, they often compare Google Maps listings, read reviews, explore treatment pages, and visit practice websites before making contact. If a practice has weak local visibility, incomplete business information, or an unclear website experience, it may miss valuable opportunities.

Over the past 2+ years, I have focused on Dental SEO, local search strategy, keyword research, Google Business Profile optimization, and patient acquisition planning. My work has helped me develop a structured approach to identifying visibility gaps and turning research into practical recommendations.

I also continue to explore how AI-powered search experiences are changing the way people discover businesses and evaluate information.

My Approach to SEO

I believe effective SEO begins with understanding the practice, its market, and its business goals.

My process follows five stages:

  1. Understand Your Practice

    Review your services, target locations, and priorities.

  2. Audit Your Online Presence

    Evaluate your website, Google Business Profile, local visibility, and competitors.

  3. Build a Strategy

    Prioritize keyword opportunities, content improvements, and technical recommendations.

  4. Support Implementation

    Implement approved improvements or provide clear guidance, depending on the agreed scope.

  5. Measure and Refine

    Review available performance data and adjust priorities based on evidence.

My goal is to make SEO more understandable, practical, and measurable for dental businesses.
