Competitor Analysis Strategy

Learn what competing practices do well and where the real gaps are

A competitor analysis strategy studies the practices that compete for the same patients in search and on Google Maps. It looks at their public websites, treatment pages, profiles and reviews to find practical gaps and opportunities.

The goal is not to copy competitors. It is to understand the market so your own priorities are better informed.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Why Every Business Needs Competitor Analysis

Know who you really compete with

Search competitors are often different from the practices you think of as rivals.

See local positioning

Maps and organic results show who patients actually see first.

Find content gaps

Missing treatment pages or questions are opportunities.

Compare trust signals

Review volume, recency and responses shape patient choice.

Choose realistic battles

Some keywords are too competitive; others are open.

Prioritize with evidence

Decisions rest on public data, not assumptions.

My Approach

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Identify Real Competitors

    I find the practices that rank for your target searches.

  2. Review Public Presence

    I compare websites, profiles and reviews.

  3. Map Content and Keywords

    I look for coverage gaps and shared opportunities.

  4. Assess Trust and Authority

    I compare credentials, reviews and link patterns.

  5. Prioritize Opportunities

    Findings become a ranked list of actions.

What Each Study Includes

  • Competitor list and selection logic
  • Comparison of pages, profiles and reviews
  • Keyword and content gap summary
  • Prioritized opportunities

Expected ROI

The return from competitor analysis is better decisions: effort goes where gaps are real and winnable.

Where the Return Comes From

Smarter priorities

You act on gaps that matter.

Less guesswork

Public evidence replaces assumptions.

Realistic targets

You avoid keywords that are out of reach for now.

Clearer positioning

You see how to stand out honestly.

What I Would Measure

  • Visibility for selected competitive keywords
  • Share of local results over time
  • Growth in coverage of priority treatments
  • Review volume and recency vs. competitors
  • Calls and inquiries from targeted pages

A Note on Results

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use competitors’ private data?

No. The analysis uses only publicly available information, and unverified observations are marked as such.

Will it help me outrank competitors?

It identifies opportunities, but rankings cannot be guaranteed.

Do you copy competitor strategies?

No. Recommendations are adapted to your practice and market.
