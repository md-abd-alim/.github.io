Backlink Authority Building Strategy

Earn credible links through relevance, not shortcuts

Backlinks are links from other websites to yours. Quality, relevant links can signal credibility, while low-quality or manipulative links can cause harm.

This strategy reviews your current links and plans safe, relevant ways to earn more.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

My Approach

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Audit Your Links

    I review quality, relevance and risk patterns.

  2. Benchmark Competitors

    I compare link sources of competing sites.

  3. Find Local Opportunities

    I list associations, partners and community sites.

  4. Plan Linkable Assets

    I identify content worth linking to.

  5. Outreach and Review

    I plan outreach and track results.

What I Would Measure

  • Referring domains and link quality
  • Branded and non-branded visibility
  • Rankings for target pages
  • Referral traffic
  • Calls and inquiries from linked pages

A Note on Results

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you buy links?

No. I do not recommend paid or manipulative links.

How many links do I need?

There is no set number. Relevance and quality matter more than quantity.

Can links be guaranteed?

No. Outreach results vary, and I cannot guarantee any link.
