Dental Office of Tampa

GBP & Local SEO Growth Strategy · Tampa, FL

Overview

This 44-page report is a Google Business Profile and local SEO growth strategy for a family dental practice in Tampa, Florida. The practice had a 5-star rating and over 100 reviews but little organic traffic and no dedicated pages for several high-value services. The report audits technical SEO and the profile, then plans Maps, service pages, North Tampa locations, trust, authority and a 90-day roadmap.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Key Findings

Highlights from the PDF report.

5-star rating from 113 reviews, but only 15 profile photos and an incomplete service list

About 44 monthly organic visits, 91 tracked keywords and no confirmed AI search mentions

Authority Score of 8 with about 2,700 backlinks from 1,100 referring domains, flagged for quality review

Invisalign in Tampa ranked around position nine and Invisalign Tampa around seventeen

Technical issues: outdated sitemap, Dentures menu linking to an implant URL, repetitive homepage title

Local focus on North Tampa, Carrollwood, Northdale and Lake Magdalene

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Market and baseline

    Mapped North Tampa communities and competitors, then established the baseline, noting strong brand rankings but weaker non-branded terms.

  2. Technical audit

    Found sitemap, navigation and URL mismatches, a repetitive title and mobile zoom limits, with Core Web Vitals and schema still needing tests.

  3. Profile and Maps

    Planned confirmed hours and details, a full service list, categories, real photos, posts and Q&A, with relevance, distance and prominence tracked by a ranking grid.

  4. Competitors, keywords, pages

    Compared five competitors, grouped keywords by intent, and planned dedicated service pages, silos and location pages for North Tampa areas.

  5. Trust, authority, roadmap

    Added E-E-A-T, honest review requests, citations, local links, AI search readiness and conversion tracking within a three-month roadmap.

What I Would Measure

  • Organic traffic
  • Keyword rankings
  • Google Business Profile calls
  • Website clicks and direction requests
  • Review count
  • Patient enquiries

Important Notes

Targets in the report are planning goals, not guarantees of ranking, traffic or revenue growth. Core Web Vitals, structured data and broken links still required testing, and services must be confirmed with the practice.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
