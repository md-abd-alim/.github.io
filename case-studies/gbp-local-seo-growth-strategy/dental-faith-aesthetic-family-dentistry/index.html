Dental Faith Aesthetic & Family Dentistry

GBP & Local SEO Growth Strategy · Nashville, TN

Overview

This 47-page report is a Google Business Profile and local SEO growth strategy for a bilingual family and cosmetic dental practice in Nashville. The practice already had about 100 blog posts, 25 service pages and a strong review profile. The report audits technical SEO and the profile, studies competitors and keywords, and plans service pages, location pages, trust, reviews and a 90-day roadmap with KPI tracking.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Key Findings

Highlights from the PDF report.

4.8-star Google rating from 271 reviews, with about 387 monthly organic visits (down 7.4 percent)

About 69.4 percent of tracked keywords informational and only 2.7 percent transactional

About 2,530 organic keywords, roughly 3,500 backlinks from 882 referring domains, Authority Score of 11

Technical issues: outdated sitemap, Dentures menu pointing to an implant URL, repetitive homepage title, mobile zoom restriction

AI visibility score of 14, with four mentions and 81 cited pages

Review target of 25 to 35 additional reviews over 90 days (planning goal)

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Market and baseline

    Assessed the local market, strong South Nashville competition near 37211, and the gap between educational traffic and bookable searches.

  2. Technical and profile audit

    Found URL, sitemap and title issues, plus profile problems: inconsistent hours, conflicting phone data, limited photos, incomplete services and an unclear booking path.

  3. Reviews, Maps and competitors

    Planned honest review requests with replies in the reviewer's language, a local ranking grid, and a competitor set that excludes similarly named businesses elsewhere.

  4. Keywords, content and structure

    Prioritized English and Spanish terms, audited overlapping blog content, and designed priority service pages, silos and location pages for Antioch, South Nashville and Brentwood.

  5. Trust, authority and roadmap

    Covered E-E-A-T, citation fixes, local links, conversion and a three-phase 90-day plan with a monthly KPI table.

What I Would Measure

  • Organic traffic
  • Transactional keywords
  • Google Business Profile calls, website clicks and direction requests
  • Review count
  • AI visibility
  • Qualified patient enquiries

Important Notes

Targets for calls, traffic, keywords, reviews and AI visibility are planning goals, not guarantees of rankings, traffic or revenue. Technical metrics such as Core Web Vitals still need testing, and doctor credentials must be verified before publication.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
