Dr. Sidney Moritz

GBP & Local SEO Growth Strategy · Miami, FL

Overview

This 29-page report is a 12-month organic growth strategy for a dental implant business in Miami, Florida. It uses SEO, Google Maps, content, reviews, authority building, E-E-A-T and AI search optimization, with no paid advertising. The plan starts with Miami, then high-value Miami areas, then Florida authority, and considers broader U.S. expansion only if local data supports it.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Market and competitors

    Studied Miami-Dade's population mix and implant patient needs, finding room to compete on cost clarity, doctor visibility, bilingual education and Maps presence.

  2. Keywords and structure

    Gave each intent one page, covering cost, full-mouth, single-tooth and denture options, with an education hub and trust pages around an implant hub.

  3. Local foundation

    Combined technical SEO, an accurate Google Business Profile, consistent citations and real-patient reviews, with no fake or gated reviews.

  4. Locations, content, authority

    Planned unique location pages, commercial pages before blog posts, and authority from profiles, interviews and publications, avoiding bulk links.

  5. Conversion, AI, expansion

    Focused on consultations, added GEO content for AI platforms, and set gated expansion decisions inside a 12-month roadmap with KPIs.

What I Would Measure

  • Keyword visibility
  • Google Maps activity
  • Organic traffic and commercial page performance
  • Calls, forms and consultation requests
  • Reviews and backlinks
  • Qualified implant enquiries

Important Notes

Month one creates the baseline before progress is measured, and no AI citation or mention can be guaranteed. Business claims must be verified before publication, and national expansion is proposed only when real demand data supports it.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
