Skelton Orthodontics

GBP & Local SEO Growth Strategy · Fort Payne, AL

Overview

This is a Google Business Profile and local SEO growth strategy for an orthodontic practice in Fort Payne, Alabama. The practice has a strong local reputation and modern technology, but all ten services sat on one page and nearby towns had no dedicated content. The report sets out a profile plan, service and location architecture, and a 90-day roadmap.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Key Findings

Highlights from the PDF report.

5-star Google rating from 126 reviews

All ten orthodontic services listed on a single services URL

About 187 monthly organic visits, 85 organic keywords, an Authority Score of 9, and an AI visibility score of 28

No dedicated pages for Rainsville, Collinsville, or Scottsboro

Mobile Text Us button pointed to an email address instead of an SMS destination

A competitor already had offices in Rainsville and Scottsboro with dedicated service and location pages

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Set the baseline

    Reviewed strengths, reviews, and visibility using supplied client data and third-party estimates, not Search Console or Analytics.

  2. Fix technical basics

    Targeted the Text Us link, generic metadata, a spelling error, and tracking before larger builds.

  3. Optimize the profile

    Kept Orthodontist as primary category, verified secondary ones, itemized real services, and planned photos, posts, and common-question answers.

  4. Close the page gaps

    Planned dedicated pages for Invisalign, braces, teen, adult, early treatment, LightForce, and retainers, plus a location silo for Fort Payne, Rainsville, Collinsville, and DeKalb County.

  5. Build trust and authority

    Consolidated two doctor biography pages, set up a no-incentive review process, and planned citations and local links.

What I Would Measure

  • Reviews
  • Organic traffic
  • Keyword visibility
  • Google Business Profile actions
  • AI visibility
  • Consultation requests

Important Notes

Ranking targets are planning objectives, not guaranteed rankings, and baseline figures come from supplied data and third-party estimates. Scottsboro, Boaz, and Albertville are later opportunities needing more local proof, and structured data cannot guarantee AI citations.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
