Weninger Dentistry, PLLC

GBP & Local SEO Growth Strategy · Tampa, FL

Overview

This is a Google Business Profile and local SEO growth strategy for a general and cosmetic dental practice in Tampa, Florida. The practice has a strong brand and a large library of content, but non-branded searches ranked weakly and some high-value pages were short. The report covers keyword, page, location, and trust strategy and a 90-day roadmap.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Key Findings

Highlights from the PDF report.

Supplied figure of 574 Google reviews at 5 stars, needing live verification

Non-branded searches like dentist in Tampa estimated at positions 14 to 22

16 percent decline in tracked keyword coverage, with the cause unconfirmed

Inconsistent phone numbers, suite number, hours, and older provider listings

Overlapping articles on snap-on dentures, Invisalign versus braces, and crowns versus veneers

19 area pages, several using similar templates with little unique local information

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Set the baseline

    Used third-party estimates, exported lost keywords, and separated branded, non-branded, and irrelevant terms.

  2. Verify business data

    Confirmed the correct phone, suite, hours, and listings so the website, profile, and directories agree.

  3. Plan Maps tracking

    Used relevance, distance, and prominence with a geographic ranking grid for implants, cosmetic, snap-in dentures, and sedation searches.

  4. Fix the page structure

    Built a keyword cannibalization map, page specifications, and service silos, and favored strengthening existing pages over adding content.

  5. Take a Tampa-first approach

    Strengthened the main Tampa page and consolidated or improved area pages only after reviewing traffic and ranking history.

What I Would Measure

  • Organic clicks and impressions
  • Non-branded rankings
  • Google Business Profile calls and directions
  • Website clicks
  • Reviews
  • Appointment requests

Important Notes

Metrics are third-party estimates, and live Google Business Profile access and first-party analytics were not available. Ranking targets are planning objectives, not guarantees, and specialist categories should not be added without verified provider qualifications.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
