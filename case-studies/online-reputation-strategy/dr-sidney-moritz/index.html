Dr. Sidney Moritz

Online Reputation Strategy · U.S. (city and state not stated in script)

Overview

This 19-page report is an online reputation and branded search strategy for a U.S. dental professional. When people searched his name, third-party news, regulatory and court pages filled much of the results, with few strong pages he controlled. The report prioritizes those results by authority and control, then proposes an authority ecosystem built from an official website, profiles, expert content and credible mentions over twelve months.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Key Findings

Highlights from the PDF report.

Branded search results were dominated by third-party pages, with no strong official source

Four-tier asset portfolio: owned authority, controlled profiles, earned authority and discovery assets

Results are prioritized by result type, authority, degree of control and potential impact

Five-part response framework for negative content, from corrections to monitoring

Twelve-month roadmap in three stages: months 1-3, 4-6 and 7-12

Working budget of 250 to 450 U.S. dollars per month, with website, hosting, media and legal review separate

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Diagnose the problem

    Found that third-party pages told most of the professional story, and that historical and current name variants were not clearly connected online.

  2. Risk map and ecosystem

    Ranked branded results by authority and control, and chose to build owned assets rather than fight high-authority pages.

  3. Official website and identity

    Planned an official professional website as the central authority asset, with consistent name, credentials, biography and structured data across the web.

  4. Profiles, content and PR

    Audited existing profiles, strengthened LinkedIn and YouTube, and planned expert content, videos and real third-party mentions, with no fake profiles or reviews.

  5. Negative content, AI and roadmap

    Set five responses to negative content, entity clarity for AI search, and a three-stage 12-month plan measured against a baseline audit.

What I Would Measure

  • Positive or neutral assets in top results
  • Visible owned assets
  • Movement of negative results over time
  • Profile consistency
  • Referring domains and content visibility
  • Accuracy of AI descriptions

Important Notes

The budget is a proposed strategy estimate, not money spent or results achieved. The script promises neither removal of third-party articles nor AI citations, and says numerical targets should be set after the baseline audit.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
