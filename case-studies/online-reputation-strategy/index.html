Online Reputation & Branded Search Strategy

Strengthening what appears when people search a dentist’s name

When patients search a dentist’s name, the first page of results shapes their view. If third-party pages dominate and nothing official ranks, the story is out of the practitioner’s hands.

This strategy shows how I assess branded search results and plan an authority ecosystem that gives the professional a stronger, accurate presence.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Why Every Business Needs a Reputation Strategy

Name searches are high-intent

People often search a provider by name before booking.

You may not control the results

News, directories and other third-party pages can fill the page.

Official sources need to rank

A clear official website and consistent profiles help establish the facts.

Responses should be planned

A calm, factual framework prevents reactive mistakes.

AI tools repeat what they find

Accurate sources reduce the chance of outdated descriptions.

Reputation takes time

A phased plan builds assets steadily and honestly.

My Approach

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Diagnose the Problem

    I map what ranks for the name and who controls it.

  2. Risk Map and Ecosystem

    I prioritize results by authority, control and impact.

  3. Official Website and Identity

    I plan the official site and a consistent identity.

  4. Profiles, Content and PR

    I outline profiles, expert content and credible mentions.

  5. Negative Content, AI and Roadmap

    I set a response framework and a 12-month schedule.

What Each Study Includes

  • A 19 page PDF strategy report
  • A branded search risk map
  • A four-tier asset portfolio plan
  • A response framework and 12-month roadmap

Expected ROI

Reputation work does not produce an instant number. Its value comes from clearer, more accurate first impressions.

Where the Return Comes From

Stronger first impression

More positive or neutral assets in top results.

Control of the story

Visible official pages and consistent profiles.

Calmer responses

A framework for dealing with negative content.

Accurate AI descriptions

Reliable sources feed AI summaries.

What I Would Measure

  • Positive or neutral assets in top results
  • Visible owned assets
  • Movement of negative results over time
  • Profile consistency
  • Accuracy of AI descriptions

A Note on Results

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you remove negative results?

No one can promise removal. The plan focuses on building credible assets and responding appropriately.

Do you recommend fake reviews?

Never. Only genuine experiences and honest, policy-compliant practices.

What is the budget?

The report uses a working budget range, with website, hosting, media and legal review separate.
