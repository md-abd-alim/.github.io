Southern Smiles Dental Center

Keyword Research to Content Roadmap · Gadsden, AL

Overview

This 11-sheet workbook is a keyword research and strategy package for a dental practice in Gadsden, Alabama, focused on dentures, same-day dentures, affordability and an on-site denture laboratory. It organizes 241 keywords into 26 clusters, reviews local competitors and maps keywords to pages. It then plans silos, internal links, a six-month content roadmap and quick wins, including handling a legacy domain.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Business analysis

    Reviewed services including dentures, same-day dentures, crowns, bridges, root canals, extractions and clear aligners, and set three goals around local visibility, high-intent patients and commercial content.

  2. Keywords and clusters

    Tagged keywords by intent, funnel stage, volume, difficulty and value, then built 26 clusters including local dentist, same-day dentures and denture cost.

  3. Competitors and mapping

    Found gaps in same-day denture, pricing, repair and insurance detail, and gave each intent a page, including a denture hub and same-day page.

  4. Authority, silos and links

    Mapped denture, restorative and extraction topics into silos with parent and supporting pages, linked by 295 internal links.

  5. Roadmap and quick wins

    Scheduled 70 items starting with homepage, denture hub, same-day page and new-patient page, and listed quick wins plus legacy-domain consolidation.

What I Would Measure

  • Rankings for the main Gadsden dentist term
  • Same-day denture page visibility
  • Denture cost keyword performance
  • Organic traffic to mapped pages
  • Calls and appointment requests
  • Content roadmap completion

Important Notes

Workbook numbers show the scope of research, not traffic or achieved rankings. Search metrics are estimates to validate before implementation, and claims such as free adjustments and legacy-domain content must be verified before publication.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
