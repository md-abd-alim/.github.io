From keyword data to a clear plan for pages, internal links and content

The workbooks are Excel files you can download and open, with 11 or 12 sheets each.

Why Every Business Needs Keyword Research Before Content

Every page needs a search purpose

Pages built without a target search compete with each other or answer nothing.

Search intent differs

Someone ready to book needs a different page from someone comparing treatments or costs.

Volume is not value

A smaller search that signals a ready patient can be worth more than a broad, high-volume term.

Overlap wastes effort

Multiple pages aimed at one search split relevance and confuse search engines.

Structure builds authority

Related services, questions and guides linked in clusters show depth on a topic.

A roadmap sets the order

You know what to optimize or publish first, instead of guessing.

My Approach

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Understand the Business

    I study services, locations, audience and the current website.

  2. Build the Keyword Database

    Each keyword is scored by intent, business value and opportunity.

  3. Cluster and Map

    Keywords are grouped into topics and mapped to specific URLs.

  4. Review Competitors and Gaps

    I note visible strengths, missing content and unverified areas.

  5. Plan Silos, Links and Content

    Architecture, internal links and a content roadmap with quick wins.

What Each Study Includes

  • Business analysis and master keyword database
  • Keyword clusters with primary and supporting terms
  • Competitor analysis and keyword-to-URL mapping
  • Topical authority map and SEO silo architecture
  • Internal linking blueprint
  • Content roadmap, quick wins and scoring methodology

Expected ROI

Keyword research pays back by pointing effort at pages that can win relevant searches. The measures below show whether the plan is working.

Where the Return Comes From

Fewer wasted pages

Each page has one clear purpose and a mapped keyword.

Better-matched traffic

Content aligns with what ready-to-book patients search for.

Faster first moves

Quick-win lists identify what deserves early attention.

Compounding value

Clusters and internal links strengthen related pages over time.

What I Would Measure

  • Search Console impressions and clicks by page
  • Rankings for priority keywords
  • Organic traffic to mapped pages
  • Calls and appointment requests from target pages
  • Content roadmap completion

A Note on Results

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the keyword metrics exact?

No. Search volume, difficulty and CPC figures are research estimates and should be validated against live search data and your own analytics.

Does a priority score guarantee rankings?

No. It is a decision tool for ordering work, not a ranking promise.

Can I download the workbook?
