Carter Family Dentistry

Keyword Research to Content Roadmap · Rainsville, AL

Overview

This 12-sheet workbook is a keyword research and strategy package for a family dental practice in Rainsville, Alabama. It organizes 136 keywords into 19 clusters by intent, business value, ranking opportunity and conversion potential. The workbook then maps keywords to pages, plans a siloed site structure and internal links, and sets out a 48-item content roadmap with a quick-wins list and a documented scoring method.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Business analysis

    Studied services (implants, crowns, root canals, dentures, veneers, emergency exams, preventive care), location and website, noting gaps in insurance, membership plan, local pages and education.

  2. Keyword database and clusters

    Tagged each keyword by type, intent, funnel stage, target URL and action, then grouped terms into 19 clusters with primary keywords and patient questions.

  3. Competitors and mapping

    Reviewed competitor positioning and content gaps, separating verified observations from open questions, and mapped keywords to single pages to limit cannibalization.

  4. Authority, silos and links

    Built supporting content around each service, organized seven silos, and planned 128 internal links from articles to service pages and location pages.

  5. Roadmap, quick wins, scoring

    Created a 48-item roadmap and a quick-wins sheet, using a weighted priority model covering value, demand, opportunity, intent, conversion and topical importance.

What I Would Measure

  • Rankings for priority keywords
  • Organic traffic to mapped pages
  • Local pack visibility
  • Calls and appointment requests
  • Content roadmap completion
  • Internal link implementation

Important Notes

Search volume, difficulty and CPC figures are research estimates to be validated against live data before implementation. Quick-win priorities and scores are strategic guides, not guaranteed rankings, and unverified competitor assumptions are not treated as fact.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
