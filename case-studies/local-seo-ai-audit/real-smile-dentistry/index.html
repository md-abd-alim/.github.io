Real Smile Dentistry

Local SEO & AI Audit · Miami Beach, FL

Overview

This is a digital presence and AI search audit for a multi-location dental practice in Miami Beach, Florida. The practice already had strong reviews, detailed service pages, and a large Instagram following. The audit found opportunities in AI search visibility, local consistency, page performance, and patient conversion. It ends with a prioritized issue list and a 30-day action plan.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Key Findings

Highlights from the PDF report.

Indexing 9 of 10, HTTPS 10 of 10, and content quality 9 of 10

5-star Google rating from 320 reviews

Four named dentists with biographies and credentials

Instagram account with about 133,000 followers and an existing YouTube channel

No confirmed Google AI Overview presence in the tracked data

Overall scores of 8 for SEO and local SEO, but only 3 of 10 for AI search

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Audit the whole presence

    Reviewed website SEO, the Google Business Profile, citations, social media, AI search, competitors, and conversion.

  2. Check technical foundations

    Noted strong indexing and structure, then flagged an autoplaying homepage video, broken implant page links, and generic or missing meta descriptions.

  3. Verify local signals

    Looked for location conflicts and flagged a possible second domain that should be confirmed as intentional or redirected.

  4. Compare with competitors

    Found one competitor with stronger reported AI visibility on fewer keywords and another ranking for more organic keywords.

  5. Prioritize quick wins

    Sequenced fixes for links, metadata, structured data on FAQs, profile services, a consented before-and-after gallery, and the appointment experience.

What I Would Measure

  • AI Overview visibility
  • Organic keyword coverage
  • Google Business Profile service presentation
  • Page performance
  • Appointment requests

Important Notes

Mentions in ChatGPT and Gemini could not be verified from the available data, and some profile details need owner access. Scores are the audit's own assessment, and the second domain should be confirmed with the practice before any change.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
