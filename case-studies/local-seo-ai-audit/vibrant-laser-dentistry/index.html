Vibrant Laser Dentistry

Local SEO & AI Audit · Colorado Springs, CO

Overview

This is a digital presence and AI search audit for a dental practice in Colorado Springs, Colorado. The practice had detailed service pages, strong credentials, consistent local listings, and working conversion tools. The audit found gaps in homepage headings and metadata, Google Business Profile activity, local authority, and AI search visibility.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Key Findings

Highlights from the PDF report.

Indexing 9 of 10, HTTPS 10 of 10, and internal linking 10 of 10

4.9-star Google rating from 86 reviews

Homepage repeats the same heading across many sections and uses a generic meta description

647 backlinks from 248 referring domains, with local relevance still to be checked

No confirmed Google AI Overview visibility; ChatGPT showed one cited page and no mentions

Scores of 7 for SEO, 8 for local SEO, 2 for AI search, and an opportunity score of 8

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Review the six areas

    Covered website SEO, the Google Business Profile, local SEO, social media, AI search, and conversion.

  2. Test the foundation

    Praised the deep service structure, then flagged repeated headings, a generic meta description, and large homepage images as a speed risk.

  3. Check the profile

    Noted the public category read Dental implants provider, to be checked in the dashboard, and that the profile had not been updated recently.

  4. Assess AI and conversion

    Found useful Q&A content to build on, plus live online booking, click-to-call, testimonials, and a smile gallery, with no live chat.

  5. Prioritize 30 days

    Rewrite metadata and headings, set a posting schedule, add structured data to FAQs, compress images, and pursue local links and LinkedIn and YouTube profiles.

What I Would Measure

  • Search performance
  • Local visibility
  • AI search visibility
  • Page speed
  • Patient enquiries

Important Notes

Primary category and eligibility must be verified through the profile dashboard before changes. Scores are the audit's own assessment, and AI visibility figures come from available tracking data.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
