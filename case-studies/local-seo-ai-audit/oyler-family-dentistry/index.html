Oyler Family Dentistry

Local SEO & AI Audit · Greendale, IN

Overview

This is a digital presence and SEO audit for a family dental practice in Indiana. The practice already had strong patient reviews, detailed service content, and visible dentist credentials. The audit found gaps in internal linking, meta descriptions, local business consistency, AI search readiness, and the path to booking. It closes with a prioritized 30-day action plan.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Key Findings

Highlights from the PDF report.

Google indexing 9 of 10, HTTPS 10 of 10, and content quality 9 of 10

Internal linking scored only 4 of 10, with broken or unreliable links on the dental implant page

Empty meta description on the implant page and a generic one on the savings program page

5.0-star Google rating from 613 reviews

Conflicting city names across the website, social profiles, and directories for one address

Overall scores of 7 for website SEO and local SEO, 6 for AI search and overall presence, with an opportunity score of 8

How I Approached It

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Review the full presence

    Looked at the website, Google Business Profile, citations, social media, AI search readiness, and conversion paths rather than rankings alone.

  2. Score and separate evidence

    Scored each area, kept verified findings apart from items that need owner access, and flagged page speed and Core Web Vitals for testing.

  3. Check local and social signals

    Compared city information across the site, listings, and social profiles, and noted existing Vimeo videos with no discoverable YouTube channel.

  4. Review AI and conversion

    Assessed question-and-answer content for AI readiness and the patient journey, including click-to-call, request forms, live chat, and a before-and-after gallery.

  5. Prioritize a 30-day plan

    Ordered fixes starting with city consistency, implant page links, and meta descriptions, then validated the changes with Search Console and analytics.

What I Would Measure

  • Search Console performance
  • Meta description and link fixes
  • Local search monitoring
  • Analytics and appointment requests
  • AI search mentions

Important Notes

Public data could not confirm several Google Business Profile details or any mentions in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, or Gemini, so those items need owner access and monitoring. The scores are the audit's own assessment, not independently verified performance.

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.
