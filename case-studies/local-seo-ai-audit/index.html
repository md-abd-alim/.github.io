Local SEO & AI Audit

Short, scored audits that show what to fix first in 30 days

A Local SEO & AI Audit is a compact review of a website, Google Business Profile and AI search readiness. Each report below is a short PDF with scored findings, a prioritized issue list and a 30-day action plan.

It is a fast, low-commitment way to see where visibility, trust and conversion are leaking before investing in a larger project.

These are independent portfolio studies based on publicly available information and third-party SEO estimates. The businesses were not contacted, are not clients and have not endorsed this work. No traffic, ranking or patient results are claimed.

Why Every Business Needs a Local SEO & AI Audit

Hidden issues cost visibility

Slow pages, weak titles and indexing gaps rarely show up until someone looks.

AI search is a new channel

AI tools summarize local businesses, and they need clear, consistent, well-structured information.

Local signals must match

The website, profile and citations should tell the same story.

Trust signals matter

Credentials, reviews and clear contact options influence whether people reach out.

You need priorities

A scored, ranked list beats a long checklist you do not know how to tackle.

A cheap, clear first step

An audit gives you a plan to act on yourself or hand to a team.

My Approach

Five stages, each with a clear purpose.

  1. Review Website and Technical SEO

    I check indexing, speed, titles, content and structure.

  2. Check Profile and Local Consistency

    I compare the Google profile, citations and local pages.

  3. Assess AI Search Readiness

    I look at content clarity, structured data and brand signals.

  4. Score and Prioritize

    Findings are scored and ranked by importance.

  5. Build a 30-Day Action Plan

    You get a short, ordered list of actions.

What Each Study Includes

  • A 7 to 8 page PDF audit report
  • Scores for SEO, local SEO and AI search
  • A prioritized issue list
  • A 30-day action plan

Expected ROI

An audit’s return comes from fixing the right problems first. I describe it in terms of what you can measure after acting on the plan.

Where the Return Comes From

Quicker fixes

A short ranked plan makes the first month productive.

Better visibility

Technical and local fixes help search engines understand and show the practice.

Clearer trust

Consistent details and credentials support patient confidence.

Informed next steps

You can decide whether a larger project is worth it.

What I Would Measure

  • Indexing, page speed and technical issue count
  • Profile views, calls and direction requests
  • Search Console clicks and queries
  • Reviews and profile completeness
  • Contact requests from the website

A Note on Results

Rankings, traffic and patient numbers cannot be guaranteed. Targets in these reports are planning goals, and the first month sets the baseline. Real return should be judged against your own data, such as the lifetime value of a new patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the audit scores mean?

They are my own assessment of the website, local presence and AI readiness at the time. They are not independently verified performance results.

Can an audit get my practice into AI answers?

No one can guarantee mentions in AI Overviews, ChatGPT or Gemini. The audit improves the clarity and consistency of the information those systems may use.

Are all these businesses dental?

No. Some audits cover other healthcare providers to show that the method works beyond dentistry. Each page says so.
